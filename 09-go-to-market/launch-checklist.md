# Launch Checklist · Hospitable Compliance Hub (Spain)

> Module 9 · Launch execution plan, same structure as the Product School launch checklist I built for a separate product-marketing project. Excel version: [`launch-checklist.xlsx`](launch-checklist.xlsx). Strategy behind it: [`positioning.md`](positioning.md).

**Product:** Hospitable Compliance Hub (Spain): permission to operate, per listing, across every channel  

**Target launch:** Full launch Q4 2027 (all Spanish regions), after the Q2 2027 test decision. Soft launch Q3 2027 in 5 regions.

_Status reflects this case study: drafts exist for strategy rows; everything else starts after discovery. Dates follow the decision gates in the [roadmap](../08-roadmap/roadmap.md)._

_____

## Context / Background

| Deliverable | Owner | Due | Status | Comments |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Positioning** | PMM Lead | Q1 2027 (Discovery) | In Progress | FOR small hosts in Spain with 1–5 listings, resident or non-resident, and the managers who run homes for them, WHO NEED to know per listing that they are legally allowed to operate right now, Hospitable Compliance Hub is the one place that shows it across every channel. Unlike each platform's own checks (one listing at a time, can delist without warning) or a gestoría (slow, paid per task, reacts after the fact), it warns hosts before a rule, a platform or a neighbours' vote takes the listing down. Draft in 09-go-to-market/positioning.md; validate wording in the 8–10 discovery interviews. |
| **Messaging** | PMM Lead | Q1 2027 (Discovery) | In Progress | Core message: · “Your licence. Every channel. Always current.” · FROM: I find out when my listing is gone · TO: I know before anything happens · Value propositions: · (1) See it once, fixed everywhere — one registration number checked and synced on Airbnb, Booking.com, Vrbo and direct · (2) Warned before, not after — alerts on draft rules, registry deadlines and community steps · (3) Proof when it counts — dated evidence vault for inspections and disputes · Tone guardrails: protection, not surveillance; never 'loophole'; never 'you are protected' (no legal advice). |
| **Pricing & Packaging** | PMM Lead + Revenue | Q1–Q2 2027 (Beta + A/B test) | In Progress | Compliance pulls hosts UP from the free plan. (1) Essentials ($0): free status view (red/amber/green per listing) + upgrade prompt when action is needed. (2) Host ($29 + $10/property): compliance core — number sync on every channel, guest-registry automation, regional and city rule alerts. (3) Professional ($59 + $15): + community status and dispute tracker, cost view (10% VAT, community and IBI surcharges). (4) Mogul ($99 + $30): + licence process tracker across owners, evidence export, multi-region view. Alternative to test: a per-property compliance add-on for Essentials, following the Dynamic Pricing add-on model. Plan prices: Hospitable help centre, Nov 2026; compliance packaging is a proposal. |
| **Competitive Intelligence** | PMM Lead | Q1 2027 (Discovery) | In Progress | Key alternatives hosts use today: (1) Spreadsheets and memory — free, no alerts, no channel view. (2) Gestoría — paid per task, reacts after the fact, doesn't see the channels. (3) Each platform's own registration checks — free but only see their own listing and can delist without warning. (4) Other host-software (PMS) competitors — compliance features to verify one by one before any claim. Watch: GuestArrive partnership (compliance automation) and any platform-native registry tools. |

## Go-To-Market Plan

| Deliverable | Owner | Due | Status | Comments |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Launch Tier** | PMM Lead | Q1 2027 (kick-off) | In Progress | Tier 2 — Feature Expansion for an existing customer base. Rolled out first to Hospitable's current Spanish hosts, then used to acquire new ones. Not Tier 1: the channel connections, inbox and guest flows already exist; the Hub adds a compliance layer on top. It is a behaviour change (checking status before acting), not a new market launch. Escalates to Tier 1 only if expanded to Portugal and other EU markets as a branded product. |
| **Target Audience** | PMM Lead | Q1 2027 (Discovery) | In Progress | PRIMARY BUYERS: small hosts in Spain with 1–5 listings, resident or non-resident (non-residents as a subgroup: most intense pain). SECONDARY BUYERS: small managers (5–50 homes). INFLUENCERS: gestorías, property administrators (administradores de fincas), host associations (Fevitur, Avaec, Arvurtur). AFFECTED: guests (registry check-in), neighbours, councils. JTBD — functional: know each listing can operate right now; emotional: stop dreading the next rule change; social: be the legal, responsible neighbour. |
| **Goals & Metrics** | PMM Lead + Analytics | Q1 2027 (Discovery) | In Progress | STRATEGIC GOALS: (1) Retain Spanish host accounts by preventing compliance-driven delistings (2) Convert Essentials users to paid plans through compliance (3) Build the compliance data behind the GoodHost Score. KEY METRICS: +25% relative lift in 30-day compliance-complete rate (primary); 90-day churn of Spanish accounts (lagging); Essentials → paid conversion among Spanish hosts; share of upgrades starting from a compliance prompt; North Star: compliant active listings. GUARDRAILS: share of Spanish listings live and bookable must not drop; time to first published listing (watch); zero auto-unpublishing. |
| **GTM Timeline** | PMM Lead | Q1 2027 (kick-off) | In Progress | PHASE 1 — Discovery (Q1 2027): 8–10 host interviews, pricing interviews, baseline compliance-complete rate, prototype usability test, legal content partner. PHASE 2 — Beta (Q1–Q2 2027): guest-registry beta (≥40% activation gate), then Compliance Hub MVP A/B test in Andalucía, C. Valenciana, Madrid, Canarias, Baleares. Beta Success Gate: scale only if ≥+25% lift and the guardrail holds (week-12 decision). PHASE 3 — Soft launch (Q3 2027): all Spanish hosts in the 5 regions, Spanish and English. PHASE 4 — Full launch (Q4 2027): all Spanish regions incl. Catalonia (own community regime). SCALE (2028): Portugal, Pro tier, GoodHost Score. |
| **Enablement Plan** | PMM Lead + Support Lead | Q1–Q2 2027 (Beta + A/B test) | Not Started | SUPPORT: playbook for compliance questions (what we do / don't do; escalation to a legal partner); macros for 'my listing was delisted'. PARTNERS: gestoría and administrador-de-fincas kit (how to read a host's evidence vault, referral link). SELF-SERVE: help-centre articles per region; 'what you lose' calculator (nightly rate × occupancy × days delisted). LEGAL: partner-verified rules with timestamps and disclaimers. BATTLE CARD: Hospitable vs spreadsheet vs gestoría vs platform-only checks. |

## Channel Plan

| Deliverable | Owner | Due | Status | Comments |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Email** | PMM / CRM | Q3 2027 (Soft launch) | Not Started | Lifecycle email + in-app to the existing Spanish base. Segment 1 — paid Spanish hosts: 'Check your listings' status in one click', personalised with their number of listings missing data. Segment 2 — Essentials users in Spain: free status view + upgrade path. Segment 3 — non-resident owners: English, German and French versions. Segment 4 — accounts with a recent delisting or registry error: priority invite to beta. |
| **Push Notifications** | PMM / Product | Q3 2027 (Soft launch) | Not Started | PRIMARY channel (unlike a B2B hotel launch): the product is the marketing. In-app and push alerts when a listing changes status, a registry deadline is close or a rule in the host's area is being drafted. Frequency capped; every alert links to a fix. One-time launch notification + follow-up if the status view hasn't been opened in 14 days. |
| **PR & Comms** | PMM / Comms | Q4 2027 (Full launch) | Not Started | Spanish housing and tourism media (El Mundo Vivienda, idealista news, Hosteltur, Expansión) + international STR trade press (Short Term Rentalz, Phocuswire). Story angle: 'Legal yesterday, illegal today: the tool that warns Spanish hosts before the rules change.' Frame as a market story (fragmented rules, the courts confirming regional powers), not a feature launch. Support with anonymised data from the beta (e.g. share of listings with a missing or mismatched number). |
| **Owned Social** | PMM / Social | Q3 2027 (Soft launch) | Not Started | Primary: Facebook host groups and Instagram (small hosts), LinkedIn (managers and partners), YouTube (explainers). Content themes: (1) 'What changed this month' regional rules digest; (2) 60-second Hub explainer; (3) beta host stories; (4) data posts (e.g. 21% of national-registry applications denied in 2025). |
| **Paid Social** | PMM / Paid | Q4 2027 (Full launch) | Not Started | Meta Ads targeting Spain-based and non-resident property owners interested in holiday rentals; LinkedIn Ads only for small managers. Creative: 'Your licence. Every channel. Always current.' + 15-second demo of the status view. CTA: 'Check your listing for free' → Essentials sign-up with the status view as the first screen. |
| **Paid Search** | PMM / Paid | Q3 2027 (Soft launch) | Not Started | High-intent Spanish queries: 'número de registro vivienda turística [región]', 'SES.Hospedajes cómo registrar viajeros', 'comunidad de vecinos piso turístico', 'licencia turística [ciudad]'. Plus English queries from non-residents ('Spain holiday rental licence'). Landing pages per region with the rules summary and a free status check. SEO content per region as the long-term engine. |
| **Out-of-home** | PMM | Q4 2027 | Not Started | Not a primary channel. Equivalent: presence at FITUR (Madrid, January) and regional host-association meetings. Small sponsorships of association events where rule changes are explained. |
| **Influencer Marketing** | PMM | Q3 2027 | Not Started | STR educators on YouTube and podcasts in Spain, host-association leaders, and gestorías that publish on regulation. Early access in exchange for honest reviews; no paid claims about legal outcomes. Disclosure required. |
| **App Install Ads** | PMM | N/A | N/A — Not Applicable | Not applicable for this launch. The Hub is a feature inside Hospitable's web and mobile app; acquisition goes through Essentials sign-up, not app installs. |
| **Word of Mouth** | Support Lead + PMM | Q3 2027 onwards | Not Started | Beta hosts as reference customers ('my listing didn't fall when the rule changed'). Referral credit for hosts who invite another host. Partner referrals from gestorías and administrators. Target NPS among Spanish hosts using the Hub: >50. |
