# The Market · STR Europe

> Module 1 · Regulatory landscape. What the rules are, who sets them, and how fast they change. As of November 2026.

## The headline

Short-term rental regulation in Europe has five layers that don't line up: the EU, the state, the region, the city and, in Spain, the building itself. Each layer can stop a host from operating. Spain is the most volatile market; Portugal is calmer nationally but tightening city by city.

_____

## EU layer

- **Data and registration (live since 20 May 2026).** Regulation (EU) 2024/1028 requires member states and platforms to run interoperable registration and data-sharing systems. Platforms must verify registration numbers and share activity data with authorities. It only bites where a country or region has set up its registration procedure, so rollout is uneven. [Source](https://www.minut.com/blog/eu-short-term-rental-regulations)
- **Affordable Housing Act (proposed September 2026).** A common method to define "housing stress" areas where authorities may restrict STRs. It is not a ban, authorities must document the impact, and primary-residence rentals are excluded. [Source](https://www.traveldailynews.com/hospitality/eu-proposes-common-framework-for-short-term-rental-restrictions/)

## Spain

| Layer | What changed | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| State | Supreme Court ruling 620/2026 (May 2026) annulled the national registry (NRUA); platform data duties survive. [Source](https://www.costaluzlawyers.com/important-update-new-short-term-rental-registration-requirements-in-spain-starting-july-2025/) | Hosts spent a year getting a number that no longer exists |
| State | Proposed 21% VAT on stays under 30 days (most are VAT-exempt today). [Source](https://www.vatupdate.com/2026/07/29/government-plans-21-vat-on-short-term-tourist-rentals/) | Depends on the 29 November 2026 election |
| State | €64M fine on Airbnb over 65,000+ non-compliant listings; court refused to suspend payment. [Source](https://www.idealista.com/en/news/node/890877) | Platforms over-correct, compliant hosts get caught in delistings |
| Building | Since April 2025, a 3/5 majority of owners can approve, limit or ban tourist use, and add up to a 20% surcharge on community fees. [Source](https://www.cronista.com/espana/actualidad-es/se-despide-la-ley-de-alquileres-las-comunidades-podran-vetar-tu-piso-turistico-y-subirte-la-cuota-hasta-un-20-tras-la-nueva-reforma) | Neighbours become a regulator |
| City | Barcelona will not renew its ~10,000 tourist licences after November 2028, backed by the Constitutional Court. [Source](https://www.catalannews.com/society-science/item/barcelona-mayor-welcomes-constitutional-court-ruling-on-tourist-apartment-restrictions) | Template for other cities |

## Portugal

- **National swing, then local power.** The 2023 "Mais Habitação" restrictions were largely reversed; Decree-Law 76/2024 handed control to municipalities. [Source](https://www.hostaway.com/blog/airbnb-rules-in-lisbon/)
- **Automatic containment.** In Lisbon, restrictions trigger automatically when the share of AL units in a neighbourhood gets too high. [Source](https://www.lvpadvogados.com/lisbons-new-al-rules-what-owners-and-investors-need-to-know)
- **Mass cancellations.** Over 10,000 AL registrations cancelled nationwide; Lisbon about 6,700, Porto 1,413. [Source](https://lisboeta.news/story/2026-06-14/over-10-000-local-accommodation-registrations-cancelled-nationwide) · [Source](https://observador.pt/2026/06/11/camaras-municipais-ja-cancelaram-mais-de-10-mil-licencas-de-alojamento-local-por-inatividade-ou-incumprimento-de-seguros/)

## Rest of Europe (for sequencing)

- **Italy:** national CIN code; Milan banning key boxes on public property. [Source](https://www.rentalscaleup.com/spain-fines-airbnb-milan-key-box-ban-forge-ai/)
- **Greece:** freeze on new registrations in central Athens and Thessaloniki extended to end-2027. [Source](https://bnbnews.gr/en/?p=42287)
- **France:** Loi Le Meur; Paris 90-night cap. [Source](https://zestinatote.com/europes-regulatory-wave-against-short-term-rentals/)

## So what

Fragmentation is not a temporary mess that a national law will fix. Spain's Supreme Court confirmed that tourism belongs to the regions, so fragmentation is permanent by design. That is the product opportunity.
