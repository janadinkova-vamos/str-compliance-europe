# The Problem · STR Europe

> Module 2 · Pain points. Political, social and operational pain points, and who feels them.

## Problem statement

Hosts want to run their rental legally, and they can't tell what "legal" means this month. One Portuguese source put it simply: running a local-accommodation business now takes constant legal monitoring, heavy administration and constant availability for the city hall. [Source](https://www.roomlala.us/blog/short-term-rentals-and-high-demand-areas-the-new-rules-boosting-long-term-rentals-in-portugal-in-2026-2778)

_____

## Political pain points

- **Rules change faster than hosts can follow:** registries created and annulled, national rules reversed, municipal rules multiplying.
- **Uncertainty by election:** Spain's 2026 snap election puts VAT, rent caps and seasonal-let rules up for grabs.
- **Platform enforcement passes risk down to hosts:** fines on platforms lead to aggressive, sometimes wrong, delistings.
- **Licences become perishable:** non-renewal (Barcelona), cancellation for inactivity or missed insurance (Portugal).

## Social pain points

- **Housing is Spain's defining political issue.** Housing and tourist flats took 63.3% of the tourism conversation in 2026. [Source](https://www.mallorca.com/en/magazin/news/spain-tourism-housing-debate)
- **Protests and stigma:** Málaga, the Canaries, Madrid and Barcelona have seen large marches; tourist flats are the visible target. [Source](https://www.theolivepress.es/spain-news/2026/06/29/thousands-protest-across-spain-amid-deepening-housing-crisis/)
- **The other side of the debate:** in Portugal, around 60,000 families depend directly on local-accommodation income. [Source](https://www.portugalresident.com/pt/Altera%C3%A7%C3%B5es-nos-alugu%C3%A9is-de-temporada-devem-prejudicar-o-setor-tur%C3%ADstico/)

## Pain points by persona

| Persona | Top pains |
|---|---|
| **Small host (1–5 listings)** | Which number is valid now; fear of surprise delisting; building votes and surcharges; manual guest-registry reporting; 10% VAT from December 2026 and possible IBI surcharges; stigma |
| **Property manager (10–500 listings)** | Tracking licence status across many municipalities; one cancelled licence = one lost owner; no portfolio-risk view; pressure to move to mid-term rentals |
| **Foreign owner** | Non-resident tax filing; distance from local rules; exposure to proposed taxes on non-EU buyers |
| **Guest** | Listings disappearing after booking; longer check-in; rising taxes; off-platform scams |
| **Neighbours and councils** | Noise and turnover; loss of housing; lack of reliable data |

## Side note: is disintermediation a threat?

Some guests find owners off-platform and pay direct. The data says platform dependence is rising, not falling: Airbnb reached about half of US professionally managed reservations in Q2 2026 while direct slipped from 26% to 23%. [Source](https://www.hostaway.com/blog/airbnb-competitors/) Off-platform payment is also the main scam vector. [Source](https://www.cpai.com/Education-Resources/Me-My-Family/Identity-Theft-tips/Booking-a-Vacation-Rental-Here-s-What-Most-Travelers-Miss) The opportunity is to turn risky leakage into safe, legal direct bookings.
